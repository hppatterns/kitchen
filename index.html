Kitchen scaler

Your recipe collection, rescaled around whatever you actually have in the fridge — with the macros keeping up.

Tap a recipe, then tell it how much of one ingredient you have. Everything else rescales to match. Per-serving calories, protein, carbs and fat stay fixed; the batch size and yield follow the amount.

Macros are estimates from a standard nutrition table — expect roughly ±10–15% depending on your exact products.
Whole-unit items (eggs, wraps, drumsticks) round to sensible amounts when you scale.
